# GTA VI artwork

The five wallpapers are official Grand Theft Auto VI art by Rockstar Games, shared here as
fan content for personal desktops. Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive own them; this
theme is not affiliated with or endorsed by either.

- `01-album-cover.jpg`: Grand Theft Auto VI, The Album cover art
- `02-golden-hour.jpg`: Jason and Lucia at sunset
- `03-grotti-cheetah.jpg`: Grotti Cheetah, Ultimate Edition
- `04-robbery.jpg`: Jason and Lucia, Robbery
- `05-lock-and-load.jpg`: Jason and Lucia, Lock and Load

`backgrounds-ultrawide/` holds Rockstar's own 21:9 versions of Robbery and Lock and Load,
used automatically on an ultrawide screen.

The palette is drawn from these pictures: dusk violet background, Vice pink accent,
sunset peach, Cheetah red, palm green, twilight blue, lavender and ocean cyan.
